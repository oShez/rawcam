RAWCAM

Android · manual RAW video

Raw sensor data. Straight to Resolve.

RawCam records uncompressed Bayer video directly off your phone's camera sensor and exports CinemaDNG you can grade like a cinema camera. Manual ISO, shutter, focus, and white balance, with no auto-anything getting in the way.

Spec

Capture
Bayer RAW off the sensor — Packed10 / Packed12 / Raw16, chosen automatically per lens
Export
CinemaDNG, dual-illuminant calibrated, parallel export pipeline
Control
Manual ISO, shutter, focus, white balance (Kelvin + tint), with per-parameter locks
Lenses
Multi-lens, including hidden telephoto/periscope lenses on supported devices
Safety
Thermal-aware auto-stop, storage-reserve guard, configurable max clip length
Requires
Android 13+, a camera with RAW capability (checked automatically at launch)

Install

  1. Download the APK above.
  2. Open the downloaded file and allow installs from this source when your phone asks. This is a one-time permission for the browser or file manager you used to download it.
  3. Grant the camera permission on first launch.
Play Protect may flag this as an unrecognized app. That's expected for anything distributed outside the Play Store, not a sign of a problem. RawCam requests zero network permissions, so nothing it does can ever reach the internet.
